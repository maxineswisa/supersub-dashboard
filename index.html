How suggestions are ranked
  1. Caregivers free during the visit who have worked with this person before and cover every care need.
  2. Caregivers free during the visit who cover every need but are new to this person.
  3. The super sub on duty that day, last. If that's you, the visit is yours once nobody above can take it.

Caregivers who don't match the client's male or female caregiver preference are left off the lists. The super sub on duty still covers, and calls the family first to let them know.

Within each group, whoever has worked the most shifts with this person comes first.
